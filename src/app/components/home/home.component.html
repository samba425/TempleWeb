About Lord Ayyappa

Lord Ayyappa, also known as Dharma Sastha, is a Hindu deity who is believed to be an incarnation of Dharma. The temple at Sabarimala, dedicated to Lord Ayyappa, is one of the most sacred pilgrimage sites in India.

Every year, millions of devotees from across the world undertake the pilgrimage to seek the blessings of Lord Ayyappa, observing strict penance and discipline.
