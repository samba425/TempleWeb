## ✨ Color Palette Upgrade

### Primary Colors
- **Deep Temple Brown**: `#2c1810` - Rich, authentic temple atmosphere
- **Sacred Saffron**: `#d4651a` - Traditional Hindu temple color
- **Fire Orange**: `#c43b00` - Energy and devotion
- **Deep Maroon**: `#8b2500` - Spiritual depth
- **Cream Background**: `#fff8f0` - Warm, peaceful base
- **Golden Accents**: `#ffd89b`, `#ffb347` - Divine radiance

### Design Philosophy
The new color scheme draws inspiration from traditional South Indian temples:
- **Saffron tones** represent devotion and spirituality
- **Deep browns** create grounding and stability
- **Golden accents** symbolize divine presence
- **Cream backgrounds** provide warmth without harshness
